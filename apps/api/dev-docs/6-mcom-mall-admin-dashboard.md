# Step 6: MCOM Mall Admin Dashboard

## Overview
Build the full 26-screen MCOM Mall Admin Dashboard — the operational command center for the entire local commerce ecosystem. This controls boroughs, high streets, businesses, customers, campaigns, promotions, gamification, QLinks, expos, rewards, memberships, audits, analytics, community, automation, moderation, billing, teams, settings, and support.

## Screens

### Screen 1 — Admin Login
Secure enterprise login with:
- Email + Password
- OTP verification modal (6-digit)
- Device recognition
- Loading & permission validation states
- Remember Device toggle

### Screen 2 — Admin Dashboard Home
Live operational command center.

KPI Cards
- Total Businesses, Active Boroughs, Active High Streets, Active Customers
- Campaigns Running, Rewards Redeemed, Active QLinks
- Expo Participation, Live Promotions, Community Engagement Score
- Each with: trend indicator, mini chart, borough comparison

Live Map Section: Interactive borough map with high street activity, QR scan heat zones, campaign hotspots

Live Activity Feed: Business approvals, rewards redeemed, promotions launched, gamification activity

Quick Action Panel: Approve Business, Launch Campaign, Activate High Street, Send Notification, etc.

Performance Charts: Borough growth, campaign engagement, QR analytics, storefront visibility, rewards usage

### Screen 3 — Marketplace Management Dashboard
Storefront ecosystem control.

Overview: Total Storefronts, Active Listings, Featured Businesses, Borough Distribution, Traffic, Conversion

Store List Table: Business Name, Borough, Category, Membership Level, Storefront Score, Visibility Rating, Status, Engagement

Filters: Borough, Category, Membership Tier, Visibility Score, Activity Level, Verification Status

Category Management: Side panel for create/edit/reorder categories, assign icons, featured placement

### Screen 4 — High Street Management Dashboard
Physical and virtual high street ecosystems.

Overview: Active High Streets, Pending Activations, Community Participation, Borough Coverage, Traffic, QR Engagement

Interactive Map: High street zones, activation zones, traffic density, engagement clusters

High Street List: Name, Borough, Activation Status, Physical/Virtual Hub, Community Group, Total Businesses, Engagement Score

### Screen 5 — High Street Activation Flow
5-step wizard:

Step 1 — Basic Details: Name, Borough, Region, Categories, Description
Step 2 — Hub Setup: Virtual/Physical/Both, Address, Manager, Contact, Hours
Step 3 — Community Creation: Group Name, Description, Engagement Goals, Visibility
Step 4 — Assign Management: Borough Managers, Moderators, Campaign Managers, Community Leaders
Step 5 — Review & Activate: Summary preview, ecosystem preview, businesses affected

### Screen 6 — Borough Management Dashboard
Local borough ecosystem control.

Overview: Active Businesses, Customer Participation, Borough Campaigns, Rewards Activity, Local Events, Engagement Rate

Borough List: Name, Population Activity, Business Count, Active Campaigns, Community Health Score

Detail Tabs: Businesses, Customers, Campaigns, Events, Rewards, Community, Analytics, Visibility

### Screen 7 — Business Management Dashboard
Onboarding, approvals, visibility, operations.

Overview: Pending Approvals, Verified, Suspended, Featured Businesses, Membership Distribution, Visibility Performance

Business Table: Name, Category, Borough, Membership Tier, Visibility Score, Verification Status, Engagement Rate, Storefront Status

Actions: Approve, Reject, Suspend, Feature, Audit, Contact

Profile Tabs: Overview, Storefront, Campaigns, Rewards, Membership, Visibility, Analytics, Activity Logs, Verification, Billing

### Screen 8 — Business Verification Screen
Secure verification review.

Left Panel: Submitted business details (name, address, phone, category, ownership)

Right Panel: Document viewer, Google Business verification, email/phone verification, fraud alerts, risk indicators

Actions: Approve, Reject, Request More Information, Escalate Review

### Screen 9 — Customer Management Dashboard
Engagement, loyalty, participation tracking.

Overview: Total Customers, Active Users, Rewards Participation, Gamification Participation, Borough Engagement, Loyalty Retention

Customer Table: Name, Borough, Membership Status, Rewards Points, Engagement Score, Last Activity, Loyalty Level

### Screen 10 — Campaign Management Dashboard
Cross-borough campaign control.

Overview: Active, Scheduled, Borough, High Street Campaigns, Campaign Engagement, Revenue

Campaign Table: Name, Borough, Status, Businesses Involved, Reach, Engagement, Start/End Date

Creation Flow: Campaign Details → Borough Selection → Business Assignment → Rewards Integration → Promotion Design → Scheduling → Review & Launch

### Screen 11 — Promotions Management Dashboard
Flash deals, seasonal, visibility promotions.

Sections: Flash Deals, Seasonal, Borough, Featured, Loyalty Promotions

Promotion Table: Name, Business, Borough, Discount, Status, Performance, Expiration

### Screen 12 — Gamification Management Dashboard
Interactive game control.

Overview: Active Games, Rewards Distributed, Daily Participants, Leaderboard Activity, Abuse Reports

Game Types: Spin Wheel, Box Rewards, Scratch Cards, Challenges, Daily Check-ins, Referral Games

Management: Create Game, Configure Rewards, Set Rules, Set Probabilities, Assign Boroughs, Launch Campaign

### Screen 13 — QLinks Management Dashboard
QR campaigns and smart destinations.

Overview: Total QLinks, Daily Scans, Conversion Rate, Active Campaigns, Top Performing Links

### Screen 14 — Expo & Promo Dashboard
Event ecosystems and business participation.

Overview: Upcoming/Active Expos, Participating Businesses, Visitor Registrations, Campaign Reach, Borough Participation

### Screen 15 — Rewards & Loyalty Dashboard

### Screen 16 — Membership Management Dashboard

### Screen 17 — Audits & Visibility Dashboard

Overview: Visibility Scores, Storefront Completion, Engagement Ratings, Borough Ranking, Campaign Performance

### Screen 18 — Analytics & Reporting Dashboard
Visual, operational, borough-focused analytics.

Charts: Borough Engagement, Storefront Traffic, Campaign Reach, QR Activity, Rewards Usage, Gamification Participation, Customer Retention

Filters: Date Range, Borough, Business Type, Campaign Type, Membership Tier

### Screen 19 — Community & High Street Activity Dashboard
Local interactions and participation monitoring.

Community Feed: Local discussions, event participation, campaign engagement, business interactions, borough conversations

### Screen 20 — Notifications & Communication Dashboard

Types: Borough Announcements, Push Notifications, Reward Alerts, Event Reminders, Emergency Notices, Platform Updates

### Screen 21 — Automation Systems Dashboard
Smart operational automation.

Types: Auto Campaigns, Scheduled Promotions, Reward Triggers, Inactivity Campaigns, Event Reminders, Borough Pushes

Automation Flow Builder: Triggers → Conditions → Delays → Notifications → Rewards → Borough Targeting

### Screen 22 — Moderation & Compliance Dashboard
Secure operational control.

Overview: Abuse Reports, Spam Activity, Suspicious QR Activity, Fake Rewards, Suspended Accounts

Actions: Review, Suspend, Warn, Resolve, Escalate

### Screen 23 — Billing & Financial Oversight Dashboard

Tables: Transactions, Membership Payments, Campaign Purchases, Credits, Refunds

### Screen 24 — Team & Staff Management Dashboard
Admin permissions and operational roles.

Roles: Super Admin, Borough Admin, High Street Manager, Campaign Manager, Moderator, Support Staff

Team Table: Name, Role, Borough, Permissions, Last Active, Status

Actions: Edit Role, Suspend, Assign Borough, Reset Access

### Screen 25 — System Settings Dashboard
Ecosystem configuration.

Categories: Membership Settings, Borough Settings, Gamification Rules, Billing Settings, Notification Systems, API Integrations, Security Settings, Automation Rules

### Screen 26 — Support & Resolution Center
Platform-wide support operations.

Overview: Open Tickets, Escalated Cases, Business Issues, Customer Complaints, Campaign Problems

Support Table: Ticket ID, User, Borough, Issue Type, Priority, Assigned Agent, Status

Actions: Respond, Escalate, Resolve, Assign Agent

- 3H — Visibility Management (overview, control table, boost modal)

- 3J — Marketplace Analytics (charts, heatmaps, export panel)

## Key UI Patterns
- Orange accent / white background / black typography design system
- Live data via WebSocket for activity feeds and map updates
- Interactive maps (boroughs, high streets, engagement zones)
- Advanced data tables with bulk actions
- Tab-based detail views
- Wizard-style multi-step flows (high street activation, campaign creation)
- Modals for quick actions (approve, suspend, feature)
- Empty states with onboarding guidance for each section
- Responsive sidebar navigation with section grouping
